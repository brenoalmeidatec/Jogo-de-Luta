MOTION: grappler idle loop, the base reference for every other sheet: wide low stance, knees bent, both open hands forward ready to grab, the crook held low in the back hand, calm half-smile, slow heavy breathing. Frames 1 to 4 he sinks slightly, 5 to 8 he rises; frame 8 flows back into frame 1. Standing tall.

SIZE: the image is 2172x724 pixels. Draw the figure at exactly the same pixel size as the attached sheet (standing, about 386 pixels from the soles to the top of the hair, soles at about y=578). Every frame sits in its own column with at least 20 pixels of empty magenta between neighbours. Do NOT draw motion lines, speed lines, impact flashes, dust or any other effect: only the character and what he holds.
